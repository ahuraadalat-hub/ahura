# Sub-Series Plan — "AI Tools for Freelancers"

A sub-series that lives *inside* the Personal Finance for Freelancers channel (see [01-niche-strategy.md](01-niche-strategy.md)). The job: capture high-volume AI search traffic without diluting the durable finance brand.

## Positioning

- **Series promise:** "AI tools that save freelancers 10+ hours a week — and the money that buys back."
- **Why it fits the channel:** every tool video ties back to a freelancer outcome (more billable hours, lower software spend, faster invoicing). It's finance content wearing an AI thumbnail.
- **Cadence:** 1 sub-series video for every 2–3 core finance videos. Enough to ride trends, not enough to become an "AI channel" that decays.
- **Format:** 6–9 min, screen-share heavy, one tool or workflow per video, always ending with the time-or-money saved.

## Monetization hooks (per video)

- Affiliate links to the tools featured (recurring SaaS commissions).
- Pin the channel's freelancer finance template/course in the description.
- Sponsor slot once a tool video proves it converts.

## Series rules (so it doesn't rot)

1. **Lead with the outcome, not the tool.** Tools change; "get paid faster" never does.
2. **Pin an evergreen finance asset in every description** so trend traffic funnels into the durable brand.
3. **Re-shoot or update the top 3 performers yearly** — AI tutorials decay; refreshing them recaptures the ranking.
4. **Never let the sub-series outnumber core finance videos.** The finance brand is the moat; AI is the lure.
