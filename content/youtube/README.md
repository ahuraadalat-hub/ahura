## How to use this pack

1. **Pick the channel.** `01-niche-strategy.md` makes the case for **Personal Finance for Freelancers** as the core brand, with **AI Productivity** as a sub-series (`05`).
2. **Shoot the anchor videos.** `02` and `03` are complete, performable scripts with `[HOOK] / [BODY] / [CTA]`, `[PAUSE]` markers, and `[VISUAL:]` tags.
3. **Ship with SEO.** `04` is the metadata package — paste titles, description, tags, and chapters straight into the upload.
4. **Build the series.** `05` and `06` give you the next 10+ videos so the channel doesn't stall after launch.

> Conventions used in every script: `[HOOK]` = first 30s scroll-stopper · `[PAUSE]` = breath/edit beat every 45–60s · `[VISUAL: ...]` = scene cut · `[CTA]` = soft, non-pushy close.
