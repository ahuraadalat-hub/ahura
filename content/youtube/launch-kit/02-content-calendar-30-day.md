# 30-Day Content Calendar

Two tracks running together: **long videos** (Tue + Fri) build watch hours toward monetization; **Shorts** (3×/week) build reach and subs fast. Every Short is a clip or teaser of a long video — make once, cut many.

## Cadence

- **Long-form:** 2/week (Tue, Fri), 6–9 min each.
- **Shorts:** 3/week (Mon, Wed, Sat), 20–45 sec, repurposed from longs.
- Total over 30 days: **~8 longs + ~12 Shorts**. Batch the scripts in 2 sittings (see `03`).

## Batching workflow

1. **Sitting 1 (week 0):** generate + edit scripts for longs #1–4. Record/assemble all four.
2. **Sitting 2 (week 2):** scripts for longs #5–8.
3. **After each long is edited:** pull 1–2 vertical clips → Shorts. Add a hook caption in the first second.
4. **Schedule everything** so publishing runs on autopilot while you make the next batch.

## Rules

- Every title leads with the **outcome or the cost of inaction**, never the topic.
- First 10 seconds of every video = the payoff teased, not delivered.
- One idea per video. Confused viewers click away.
