# Channel Setup

Everything to get the channel live and credible on Day 1. Faceless-friendly — none of this requires you on camera.

## Channel name

**Recommended: The Freelance Ledger**

Why: "Ledger" signals money/finance instantly, it's brandable, evergreen, and not tied to a trend or a year. Works as a logo, a handle, and a future product name ("The Freelance Ledger Templates").

Backups if the handle is taken:
- Freelance & Funded
- The Solo Money Lab
- Paid & Independent
- Freelance Finance Fix

**Handle:** `@thefreelanceledger` (check availability; keep it identical across YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, X).

## Avatar / logo

- Simple wordmark: "FL" monogram or an open-ledger/book icon + a coin.
- Two colors max. Deep green (`#0B6E4F`) + off-white. Green = money + calm + trust, reads well at thumbnail scale.
- Must be legible as a 32px circle. Test it tiny before committing.

## Banner (2560×1440, safe area 1546×423)

- Left third: channel name + tagline.
- **Tagline:** "Money systems for people who work for themselves."
- Right: upload cadence — "New videos Tue + Fri."
- Keep critical text inside the safe area or it gets cropped on mobile/TV.
