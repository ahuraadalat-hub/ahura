# Launch Plan — The Freelance Ledger

The master plan for launching and growing a **Personal Finance for Freelancers** channel. Everything else in `launch-kit/` is execution detail off this file.

## The honest version (read this first)

No one can guarantee a channel "blows up." What *can* be engineered is the probability: pick a niche with demand + advertiser money (done — finance for freelancers), publish consistently, optimize the click and the retention, and stack income beyond ads. Do that for 90 days and you've given yourself a real shot. Most channels fail because they quit before video 30, not because the strategy was wrong.

**Monetization gates are non-negotiable** (YouTube Partner Program):
- **1,000 subscribers**, AND
- **4,000 valid public watch hours** in the last 12 months, OR **10M Shorts views** in 90 days.
- Then you apply, get reviewed, and ads/memberships/Super Thanks unlock.

So the plan runs on two tracks at once: **long videos** build watch hours (the durable money), **Shorts** build reach and subs fast.

## The 90-day roadmap

| Phase | Days | Goal | Focus |
|---|---|---|---|
| **0 — Foundation** | 1–7 | Channel live, branded, first video up | Setup (`01`), trailer, lead magnet |
| **1 — Volume** | 8–30 | 12+ long videos, 20+ Shorts published | Batch scripts (`03`), publish daily-ish |
| **2 — Signal** | 31–60 | Find your 2–3 winners, double down | Read retention, remake what worked |
| **3 — Monetize** | 61–90 | Hit/approach YPP gates, launch first product | `06` monetization roadmap |

## Success metrics (what to actually watch)

- **Click-through rate (CTR):** aim 4%+ early, 6%+ is strong. Driven by title + thumbnail.
- **Average view duration / retention:** aim 50%+ on long videos. Driven by hook + pacing.
- **Subs per video:** rising trend matters more than any single number.
- **Email signups** from the free lead magnet — this is the asset you *own*, not rented from the algorithm.

## The one rule

Lead with the **outcome**, never the topic. Not "Freelance taxes explained" — "Stop overpaying tax as a freelancer (do this once)." People click outcomes.
