# Monetization Roadmap

AdSense is the floor, not the ceiling. The finance-for-freelancers niche has the deepest stack in YouTube — high ad CPMs *plus* a clear path to products you own. Build the stack in phases so income doesn't depend on the algorithm.

## The income stack (build bottom-up)

| Layer | Starts when | Why it works here |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Lead magnet (email list)** | Day 1 | Free "Freelancer Money Starter Kit" — you own this audience forever |
| **2. Affiliates** | Day 1 (soft) | Tax software, business banking, bookkeeping tools — recurring commissions |
| **3. AdSense (YPP)** | 1k subs + 4k hrs | Finance has some of the highest CPMs on YouTube |
| **4. Digital products** | ~500+ emails | Templates, calculators, a paid mini-course — high margin, made once |
| **5. Coaching / services** | When asked | 1:1 or group help — highest $/hour, validates the course |
| **6. Sponsorships** | When numbers talk | Fintech/SaaS brands pay well to reach freelancers |

## Phase 0 — Day 1 (before you're monetized)

**Lead magnet first.** This is the most important early asset. Build the **Freelancer Money Starter Kit**:
- Tax set-aside calculator (the 25–30% rule from script #1)
- Rate calculator (the pricing video)
- 4-account money-system template
- Simple income/expense tracker

Offer it free in exchange for an email. Put the link in every description + pinned comment. Tools: a free email platform + a simple landing page or a Google Drive folder to start.

**Affiliates, softly.** Sign up for affiliate programs for tools you actually use (accounting software, business bank accounts, invoicing apps). Mention them only where genuinely relevant. Disclose clearly. Recurring SaaS commissions compound.

## Phase 1 — Hit the YPP gates

Targets: **1,000 subs + 4,000 watch hours** (longs do the heavy lifting; see `05`). When you qualify:
- Apply to the YouTube Partner Program.
- Turn on ads, channel memberships, Super Thanks.
- Finance CPMs mean even modest views pay meaningfully more than most niches.

## Phase 2 — Productize (the real money)

Once you have a few hundred email subscribers and know which topics resonate:
- **Bundle the free templates into a paid pack** ($15–$47). The free kit proves the quality; the paid pack is the complete system.
- **Mini-course** ($97–$197): "The Freelancer Money System" — the channel's best videos, structured, with the templates. You've already scripted the curriculum in your videos.
- Launch to the email list first, then mention in videos.

## Phase 3 — Scale the per-hour value

- **Coaching:** offer 1:1 or small-group sessions to your most engaged viewers. Highest hourly rate, and it tells you exactly what to build next.
- **Sponsorships:** once your numbers are solid, fintech and SaaS brands targeting freelancers will pay for integrations. Only promote what you'd recommend free — trust is the whole asset in finance.

## Realistic revenue picture

No promises — but the *shape* of it: a finance channel clearing modest views can out-earn a much bigger entertainment channel because the CPMs are high *and* the back-end products sell to a high-intent audience. The order matters: **email list → trust → products** is what turns subscribers into income. Most creators skip straight to selling and wonder why nobody buys.

## The one-line strategy

Give away the *what* for free (videos + starter kit), charge for the *done-for-you* (templates, course, coaching). Trust is the funnel; the email list is the engine.
