# Growth Playbook — First 1,000 Subscribers

The first 1,000 is the hardest stretch. The algorithm has little to work with, so you make your own momentum. Tactics below, ordered by leverage.

## 1. Shorts are your reach engine

New channels grow fastest when Shorts pull strangers in and longs convert them to subscribers and watch hours.
- Post a Short 3×/week, each repurposed from a long (see `02`).
- **Hook in the first second** — a bold caption + a claim ("This tax mistake costs freelancers thousands").
- End every Short with a reason to go deeper: "Full system on the channel."
- Shorts get views fast but low watch-hours — that's fine, they're for *discovery*, longs are for *monetization*.

## 2. Nail the click before you chase views

Don't publish 50 mediocre videos. Publish, then **study CTR and retention** on each:
- CTR under 4%? The title/thumbnail is the problem — swap them, YouTube re-tests.
- Retention drops a cliff at 0:30? The hook over-promised or the intro dragged.
- One video outperforms? **Make three more like it.** That's the whole game — find the vein, mine it.

## 3. Answer real questions (search-driven growth)

Finance is search-heavy and evergreen. Make videos that answer exact questions people type:
- "how much should a freelancer save for taxes"
- "do freelancers pay quarterly taxes"
- "how to set freelance rates"
These rank and pull steady traffic for *years*, unlike trend videos. Half your calendar should be search-intent titles.

## 4. Go where freelancers already gather

Be genuinely useful, not spammy:
- Reddit (r/freelance, r/Upwork, r/selfemployed), relevant Facebook groups, Discords.
- Answer a money question in full, *then* mention "I made a video walking through this" with a link when it genuinely fits.
- One real, helpful comment beats fifty drops.

## 5. Consistency beats intensity

The single biggest predictor of channel survival is **not quitting.** Two longs + three Shorts a week, every week, for 90 days. Batch so a bad week doesn't break the streak. The channel that's still uploading at video 50 beats the more talented one that stopped at 12.

## 6. Build the asset you own

Views are rented from the algorithm. **Email is yours.** Put the free starter kit (lead magnet) in every description and pinned comment. Even 200 emails is a launch list for your first paid product (`06`). This is how a channel becomes a business instead of a slot machine.

## What NOT to do

- ❌ Buy subs/views — poisons your reach and risks the channel.
- ❌ Chase unrelated viral trends — brings the wrong audience that never converts.
- ❌ Obsess over one video's numbers — judge on 30-video trends.
- ❌ Wait for "perfect." Published and improving beats perfect and unpublished.

## 30-day targets (realistic, not guaranteed)

| Metric | Modest | Good |
|---|---|---|
| Longs published | 8 | 8 |
| Shorts published | 10 | 12+ |
| Subscribers | 50–150 | 300–700 |
| Avg view duration | 40% | 55%+ |
| Email signups | 20 | 100+ |

Hitting "good" consistently is what puts the YPP gates (1,000 subs / 4,000 hours) in reach inside ~6 months.
