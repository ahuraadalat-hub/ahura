# SEO & Thumbnails

The click is half the battle. CTR is driven almost entirely by **title + thumbnail**; everything below is reusable across every upload.

## Thumbnail rules

- **3-word rule:** max 3–4 huge words. If it doesn't read in a 0.5s glance on a phone, it fails.
- **One focal point.** A number, a face/expression, or a single object — not a collage.
- **Contrast the title, don't repeat it.** Title says the topic; thumbnail says the emotion or the stakes.
- **Color discipline:** the brand green + one high-contrast accent (yellow or red) for the thing that matters.
- **Test tiny:** shrink to phone size before publishing. Most thumbnails die at small scale.

## Title formula (reusable)

Lead with outcome or cost-of-inaction, add specificity, hint at ease:
- `Stop [bad outcome] as a Freelancer (Do This Once)`
- `The [number] [thing] Every Freelancer Needs`
- `How to [desirable result] Without [the usual pain]`

Avoid: vague nouns ("Freelance taxes explained"), clickbait you don't pay off (kills retention + trust).

## Per-upload SEO checklist

- [ ] Keyword in the **title**, naturally
- [ ] Keyword in the **first line** of the description
- [ ] Keyword spoken in the **first 30s** (YouTube transcribes audio)
- [ ] Custom thumbnail, tested at phone size
- [ ] Chapters added (boosts retention + search)
- [ ] End screen → next video + subscribe
- [ ] Pinned comment with the lead-magnet link
- [ ] 1–2 Shorts queued from this video
