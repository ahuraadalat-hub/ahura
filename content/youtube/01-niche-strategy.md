# Niche Strategy & Ranking

Three niche ideas scored on repeatability, audience size, and monetization depth.

| Criteria | Personal Finance for Freelancers | AI Productivity Tools | Budget Travel SE Asia |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Repeatability (1–10)** | **9** — taxes, invoicing, irregular income, retirement, pricing, client horror stories. Evergreen + endless angles. | **7** — high volume but tool-dependent; churns fast, half-life of a "best AI tool" video is weeks. | **6** — strong but geographically capped; risks becoming a vlog, not a system. |
| **Audience Size** | **Mid** — ~70M US freelancers, growing, underserved vs. generic "personal finance." | **Broad** — massive, but saturated and crowded with low-trust hype channels. | **Mid → Micro** — large travel interest, but "SE Asia + budget" narrows it. |
| **Monetization Depth** | **Deepest.** Affiliates (tax software, banking, bookkeeping), digital products (templates, courses), coaching, high-CPM finance ads, sponsorships. | **Medium-High.** Tool sponsorships + affiliates are lucrative but volatile; products harder (info goes stale). | **Medium.** Affiliates (gear, booking, insurance), sponsorships, maybe a paid guide. Lower CPMs. |
| **Risk** | Low — recession-proof, you control the IP. | High — platform/tool dependency, fast decay, intense competition. | Medium — seasonality, content requires travel/cost. |

## 🏆 Recommendation: Personal Finance for Freelancers

It wins on the two criteria that compound over time — **repeatability** and **monetization depth**. Finance carries the highest ad CPMs *and* the clearest path to owning your audience (templates → course → coaching), so you're not renting your income from sponsors or AdSense. The topic is evergreen, so videos you publish today still earn in two years — the opposite of AI tools, where a #1 video can be obsolete in a month.

**Smartest play:** lead with **Finance for Freelancers** as your core, and use **AI Productivity Tools** as a *sub-series* ("AI tools that save freelancers 10 hrs/week"). You ride the AI search traffic while keeping the durable, high-trust finance brand. Budget Travel is the weakest as a primary — keep it as a personal channel, not a business.
